01 // Profile

Researcher, builder, and field-tested leader

K.A.J. PERRY // MODELED

Dr. Perry is an artificial intelligence researcher, machine learning engineer, and lead data scientist who builds AI systems from the ground up rather than sketching them on a whiteboard. He writes the code, tunes the models, and validates results against live production data himself, fitting large language models, vector databases, graph databases, and orchestration frameworks into architectures that hold up under real operational load.

Fraud detection systems he engineered for federal agencies cut false positives by more than 30 percent while processing over one billion data rows in real time, with model accuracy exceeding 92 percent in live deployment. As founder of UltraMassive Advanced Scientific Research Corp, he leads the Autotelic Routing Engine and the AperX Epistemic Hyperledger, carrying that same test-it-before-you-trust-it discipline into deep-tech product engineering.

Before data science, he served twenty one years as a Special Forces Operations and Intelligence Sergeant with the 75th Ranger Regiment and 3rd Special Forces Group (A), directing units through combat and contingency operations in nineteen countries. There he co-developed original statistical techniques to interdict enemy direct fire, an early expression of the same problem-solving instinct he later brought to machine learning.
